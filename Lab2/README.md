# Lab 2 — Agile Backlog Creation & Sprint Simulation in Jira

## Problem Statement
**#49 — Webhook Ingestion & Retry Mechanism Hub**

## Objective
Create and manage an Agile backlog in Jira using Epics, User Stories,
Story Points, and Sprint planning.

The lab involves simulating development sprints and tracking the
progress of the planned work using Jira.

## Work Completed

- Created a Jira backlog with **6 Epics and 14 User Stories**
- Assigned Fibonacci-based Story Points
- Planned and simulated Sprint 1
- Planned and simulated Sprint 2
- Tracked stories through To Do, In Progress, and Done
- Generated and analyzed the Sprint 2 Burndown Chart
- Documented the sprint planning and reflection

## Sprint Summary

### Sprint 1
- 5 stories
- 23 Story Points
- Focus: Core webhook ingestion, forwarding, and retry functionality
- All planned stories completed

### Sprint 2
- 7 stories
- 26 Story Points
- Focus: Logging, Dead-Letter Queue management, endpoint management,
  and manual recovery
- All planned stories completed

### Remaining Work
Two larger non-functional stories, 6.1 and 6.2, remained in the backlog
as future work.

## Reflection

The story points were estimated using Fibonacci values based on the
relative complexity of each story.

The backlog was prioritized so that secure webhook ingestion and
reliable delivery were handled before operational and recovery features.

The Sprint 2 burndown showed that the planned 26 Story Points were
completed ahead of the guideline. However, since the sprints were
simulated, this does not establish a sustainable long-term team velocity.
